# Competitor snapshot — Subscription Watchdog (Oct 2026)

Sources: Rocket Money help center (pricing/premium pages, Sep–Oct 2026), OneMain MyMoney help center, Apple App Store (Bobby), subby.online comparison guide (verified Aug–Oct 2026). Pricing changes often; re-verify before quoting publicly.

| Product | Model | Pricing | Key features | Gaps / weaknesses |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Rocket Money** (US) | Bank/card linking via aggregator | Free tier; Premium "pay what you think" ~$7–14/mo (7-day trial); Premium+ ~$15/mo | Auto-detects subscriptions, concierge cancellation (2–10 days), bill negotiation (fee = % of savings), budgets, credit score, net worth | Requires bank login; heavy upsell; cancellation is a paid concierge; broad "finance app" rather than focused tool; privacy concerns with aggregator access |
| **OneMain MyMoney (formerly Trim)** | Bank linking | Free; bill negotiation exclusive to OneMain/Brightway customers | Subscription monitoring, cancellation requests, spending alerts, budgeting | Owned by a lender (cross-sell incentive); US-only; transition from Trim confusing; still needs bank credentials |
| **Bobby** (iOS) | Manual entry | Free up to 4 subs; one-time IAPs $0.99–$2.99 | Clean tracker, reminders, hundreds of templates | Apple-only; no detection — user must remember what they pay for (defeats "forgotten subscription" use case); no price-hike detection |
| **Subby** (iOS/Android) | Manual entry | Free w/ ads; one-time PRO | Reminders, totals, optional backup | Manual; no statement import; no cancel guides of note |
| Others (SubTracker, ReSubs, Orbit, SubPilot) | Mostly manual; Orbit imports CSV/screenshots on-device; SubPilot has 500+ cancel guides | Freemium / one-time / subscription | Privacy-first positioning is emerging (SubTracker "never links to your bank", Orbit "stays on device") | Mostly mobile-only; little or no automatic price-hike detection; detection from statements is rare |

## Takeaways
1. **Two camps:** bank-linked auto-detection (Rocket Money, MyMoney) vs. manual trackers (Bobby, Subby). Few products do *automatic detection without a bank login* — that is the gap.
2. **Privacy is a real wedge:** aggregator logins are the #1 objection; new entrants already market "no bank link". CSV/paste import + delete-my-data is a credible, cheap-to-build stance.
3. **Price-hike detection is underserved:** competitors list subscriptions but rarely highlight "Netflix went $15.49 → $17.99".
4. **Cancellation help is monetized** by Rocket Money as concierge; a free, good self-serve cancel-guide library is table stakes for manual apps (SubPilot) and a differentiator vs. paywalled concierge.
5. **Pricing anchors:** one-time $1–$10 for manual apps; $7–15/mo for bank-linked. A web tool with free core + low one-time/annual "Pro" fits between.

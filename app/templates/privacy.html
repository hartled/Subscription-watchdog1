Privacy

The short version

  • We never ask for, accept or store bank usernames, passwords or account-linking tokens. You give us a file or pasted text, nothing more.
  • We keep only what's needed to find subscriptions: each transaction's date, description and amount. Other CSV columns (balances, categories, memos, account numbers) are discarded at upload. Long digit sequences in descriptions (card/account/reference numbers) are masked before saving.
  • Your data is tied to a random, anonymous browser cookie — no account or name, and no email unless you opt in to email recovery (below).
  • Data is deleted automatically after {{ retention }} days of inactivity, or immediately when you click Delete my data (bottom of every page). Deletion removes the database rows and SQLite overwrites the freed space.
  • No ads, no third-party analytics or trackers, no selling or sharing of data. We don't send marketing emails or newsletters; the only email we ever send is a recovery link you explicitly ask for.
  • Reminders are delivered only inside this app and via a calendar file you download yourself.
  • Payments (optional Unlock): handled entirely by Stripe on Stripe's own checkout page. We never see or store your card details. Stripe may ask for your email to send a receipt; we don't store it.

If you buy the Unlock

  • We store only a purchase record: the Stripe checkout session ID, the payment reference (used to honour refunds), a one-way hash of your unlock code, the date, and whether it was refunded. No name, email or card data.
  • Optional email recovery{% if not email_recovery %} (not available yet){% endif %}. Only if you opt in, we keep a keyed one-way fingerprint (HMAC-SHA256) of your email address, linked to your purchase record — not the address itself. When you ask us to re-send your link, you type your email again; we match the fingerprint and send the link to that address. The email contains only your recovery link — never card or payment details. We can't read your address back, show it to anyone, or email you anything else. Remove it any time with Delete my recovery email on the pricing page; it's also removed by Delete my data (in a browser holding your unlock) and when a purchase is refunded. To deliver the message we hand your address to our email-sending provider at that moment only.
  • Your unlock code is shown to you once after checkout and kept in a cookie in your browser. Anyone with the code can use your unlock, so keep it private.
  • Stripe processes your payment under Stripe's privacy policy. They see what you type at checkout; we don't.
  • Saved history is opt-in. Unless you turn it on, your data follows the same {{ retention }}-day auto-delete as everyone else's. If you turn it on, your transactions and reminders are kept until {{ saved_retention_days }} days without a visit, or until you turn it off or click Delete my data. They're tied to your unlock code, not to your identity.
  • Deleting your data removes transactions, reminders, saved history and any recovery email. We keep the purchase record (no personal data) so your unlock and refund rights keep working.

Cookies

Strictly necessary cookies only, all HttpOnly: sw_session (random ID for your uploaded data), sw_csrf (random anti-forgery token for forms), and if you buy the Unlock, sw_unlock (your unlock code) and briefly sw_checkout (links a checkout to your browser). Clearing sw_session disconnects you from your data, which is then auto-deleted.

Security

Use the hosted version only over HTTPS. Uploads are limited to 2 MB. Don't upload files containing more than you need — most banks let you export just a date range.
